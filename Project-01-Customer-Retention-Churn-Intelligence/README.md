Customer Retention & Churn Intelligence Platform

Business Problem: Customer churn directly impacts revenue and long-term profitability.

The objective of this project was to identify churn drivers, quantify revenue at risk, and provide actionable retention recommendations for leadership teams.

Dataset: Telco Customer Churn Dataset

Rows: 7,043
Columns: 21

Business Questions:
1. What is the current churn rate?
2. Which customer segments are most likely to churn?
3. How much revenue is at risk?
4. What actions should leadership take?

KPIs:
Active Customers
Churn Rate
Revenue at Risk
Revenue at Risk %
Churn by Contract Type
Churn by Tenure
Churn by Payment Method
Churn by Internet Service

Key Findings:
1. Month-to-Month customers demonstrated the highest churn risk.
2. Early-tenure customers exhibited significantly higher churn rates.
3. Electronic Check users showed elevated churn behavior.
4. Fiber Optic customers represented the highest churn-risk internet segment.
5. Revenue at Risk represented 30.5% of total revenue exposure.

Recommendations:
1. Promote long-term contracts.
2. Improve first-year customer engagement.
3. Review Electronic Check payment experience.
4. Investigate churn among Fiber Optic customers.
5. Implement targeted retention campaigns for high-risk segments
