Customer Retention & Churn Intelligence Platform

Business Problem: Customer churn impacts revenue and profitability. The objective was to identify churn drivers and quantify revenue at risk.

Tools Used:
Power BI
DAX
Data Modeling

Key Findings:
Month-to-month customers demonstrated the highest churn risk.
Electronic Check users showed elevated churn.
Early-tenure customers were significantly more likely to churn.
Revenue at Risk represented 30.5% of revenue exposure.
