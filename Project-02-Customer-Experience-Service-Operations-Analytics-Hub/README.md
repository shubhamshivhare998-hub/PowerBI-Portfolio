Customer Experience & Service Operations Analytics Dashboard

The objective of this project was to develop a Customer Experience & Service Operations Analytics Dashboard that provides 
leadership teams with visibility into operational performance, customer satisfaction trends, workforce effectiveness, and 
service delivery health.


Dataset Information:
Dataset Type: Customer Support Operations

Records: 5,000+ Support Tickets

Customers: 300+

Time Period: Jan 2025 – Oct 2026

Data Model:

1 Fact Table
8 Dimension Tables

Additional Challenge: The dataset intentionally contained data quality issues which were identified and already resolved 
during data preparation.:

1. Duplicate Records
2. Missing Values
3. Invalid Foreign Keys
4. Standardization Issues

Key Findings
1. Average CSAT Score remained close to 3.0 across support channels and regions.
2. Survey participation exceeded 82%.
3. SLA Achievement averaged 79.01%.
4. First Contact Resolution averaged approximately 90%.
5. Open Tickets represented 33.31% of the total workload.
6. Ticket volume remained relatively stable across reporting periods.
7. Significant performance variation existed across managers and support teams.
8. High-performing agents demonstrated stronger SLA and FCR compliance.

This project demonstrates how customer support data can be transformed into actionable operational intelligence through
data cleaning, dimensional modeling, DAX calculations, workforce analysis, and customer experience reporting using Power BI.
