CRM Sales Intelligence & Revenue Performance Platform

Business Problem: Sales leadership teams require visibility into revenue performance, pipeline health, product performance, customer contribution, agent effectiveness, and manager performance. 

The objective of this project was to create an end-to-end sales intelligence solution that enables leadership teams to make data-driven decisions.

Key KPIs:

1. Total Customers
2. Deals Handled
3. Closed Deals
4. Close Deal %
5. Sales Revenue
6. Open Opportunities

Key Findings:

1. GTX product family drives the majority of business revenue.
2. Melvin Marxen is the highest-performing manager with $2.25M revenue.
3. Darcel Schlecht is the highest-performing sales agent with $1.15M revenue.
4. Open opportunities represent 23.74% of total opportunities.
5. Revenue growth demonstrates significant month-to-month volatility.
